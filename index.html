The problem

  1. AIntermittent solar output

    A passing cloud can cut PV output within minutes, and the gas turbine has to cover the gap.

  2. BExcessive valve movement

    Chasing every fluctuation keeps the IGV valve in near-constant motion, which wears it out.

  3. CReactive control

    PID acts only after frequency has already drifted.

How we solve it?

Predictive control.

MPC-901 plans setpoints 24 hours ahead and sends them to the governor/DCS already in place. No new hardware. If the link drops, control falls back to PID.

PID + MPC: smoother setpoints
