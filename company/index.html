What happened to power plants in the future of renewable energy?

Why it matters

Renewable Energy Intermittency

The weather decides the output

The output of PV and wind turbines depends on unpredictable weather, while 96.1% of projected renewable energy is PV and wind.

Source: IEA, Renewables 2025 — Renewable electricity

>600 Microgrids in Indonesia

An archipelago of isolated grids

More than 600 isolated microgrids operate across Indonesia’s islands.

Source: Climate Impact Innovations, “Indonesia’s Grid Fragmentation: Why HVDC Transmission Is the Key to Clean Energy Transition”

56% Excess Energy on Microgrid

Energy produced, but not used

In a microgrid configuration of existing components (36 kWp PV, representing renewable generation, and a 288 kWh BESS), plus a 5 kW wind generator and a 20 kW diesel generator (representing fossil energy) scheduled to run 24 hours a day, excess energy reached 56%.

Source: Gharibvand et al., 2024, Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews, DOI 10.1016/j.rser.2024.114632

How Entropi solves it?

Predictive control.

How it works

Four steps that repeat every control cycle. Operators keep full visibility and control.

  • Reads the 24-hour weather and load forecast, including when clouds will cover the PV.

  • Plans setpoints within the turbine’s operating limits.

  • Sends setpoints only, to the existing governor/DCS.

  • The plan is refreshed every cycle; if the link drops, control falls back to PID. The operator keeps full visibility and control.

Who is Entropi?

Vision

Power plants that move with the weather, not against it.

Mission

To make solar-gas captive plants steadier and more economical by adding predictive control to the equipment operators already run: no new hardware, with PID always as the safety net.
